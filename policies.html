This Privacy Policy explains how Shubh Imaging Centre (referred to as "we", "us", or "our"), located at 102, R.K. Prime Plus, Near Balaji Hall, 150ft Ring Road, Rajkot — 360001, Gujarat, India, collects, uses, and safeguards your personal and medical information in accordance with applicable Indian healthcare and data protection laws.

01Information We Collect

When you register as a patient, book an appointment, or contact us, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal identifiers: Full name, date of birth, gender, government ID (Aadhaar, etc.)
  • Contact details: Phone number, email address, residential address
  • Medical history: Previous diagnoses, medications, allergies, and symptoms relevant to your diagnostic test
  • Diagnostic results: Imaging scans (MRI, CT, X-Ray), lab reports, sonography findings, and pathology results
  • Payment information: Transaction records (we do not store card/UPI credentials)
  • Referring physician details: Doctor's name and contact, when provided
  • Website usage data: Pages visited, browser type, approximate location (for analytics only)

02How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Scheduling, confirming, and managing appointments
  • Performing and reporting on diagnostic tests you request
  • Communicating test results to you and/or your referring physician
  • Processing payments and maintaining billing records
  • Sending appointment reminders via SMS or WhatsApp
  • Improving the quality of our diagnostic services
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations

We will never use your data for unsolicited marketing from third parties, nor sell your information to any external party.

03Medical Data & Confidentiality

All medical information — including imaging results, lab reports, and health history — is treated as strictly confidential. Access to your medical records is limited to:

  • Radiologists and pathologists directly involved in your care
  • Your referring physician (only if you have explicitly authorised sharing)
  • Administrative staff with a legitimate need for billing or scheduling
Your diagnostic reports are your property. You may request a physical or digital copy at any time by contacting our front desk or emailing us at Shubhimagingcentre@gmail.com.

04Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you authorise us to share results with a specific doctor or hospital
  • Legal obligation: When required by court order, government authority, or applicable Indian law (e.g., public health reporting)
  • Medical emergency: When your safety or the safety of others requires disclosure
  • Service providers: Trusted IT vendors who help us operate our systems, under strict confidentiality agreements

05Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encrypted digital storage for all patient records and imaging files
  • Password-protected access systems with role-based permissions
  • Physical security at our premises (CCTV, restricted access to server areas)
  • Regular staff training on data confidentiality
  • Secure deletion of records after statutory retention periods

While we take all reasonable precautions, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you not to share your patient ID or report access credentials with others.

06Cookies & Website Usage

Our website uses minimal, non-intrusive technologies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function (form submissions, page navigation)
  • Analytics: Aggregated, anonymised data to understand how visitors use the site — no personal data is collected

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. This will not affect your ability to access our diagnostic services in person.

07Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal and medical data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate information in your patient record
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent to specific uses of your data at any time
  • Receive a copy of your diagnostic reports in digital or physical format

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 10 below.

08Children's Privacy

We provide diagnostic services to patients of all ages, including minors. For patients under 18 years, a parent or legal guardian must provide consent for the procedure and is responsible for any information submitted on the minor's behalf. We treat all minor patient data with the same — or greater — level of protection as adult data.

09Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For significant changes, we will notify registered patients via SMS or email. Continued use of our services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

02Services Offered

Shubh Imaging Centre provides the following diagnostic services:

  • MRI Scan (Magnetic Resonance Imaging)
  • CT Scan — 32-Slice Computed Tomography
  • Digital X-Ray
  • Sonography / Ultrasound (USG)
  • Pathology Laboratory Tests (blood work, tissue analysis, and panels)
  • Comprehensive Health Checkup Packages

All services are provided for diagnostic purposes only and do not constitute medical advice or treatment. Our radiologists provide reports that should be interpreted by your treating physician.

03Appointments & Cancellations

Appointments can be booked by phone, walk-in, or through our website contact form. Please note:

  • Appointments are subject to availability and confirmation by our staff
  • We request at least 2 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule
  • Walk-in patients are accommodated based on availability; appointments are given priority
  • Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled appointment for registration
  • Bring your referring doctor's prescription, previous reports (if any), and a valid photo ID
  • For MRI and CT scans, specific preparation instructions will be provided at the time of booking

04Patient Responsibilities

As a patient, you are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate, complete, and up-to-date personal and medical information
  • Disclosing any known allergies, implants (pacemakers, metal plates, etc.), pregnancy, or claustrophobia before MRI or CT procedures
  • Following all pre-procedure preparation instructions provided to you
  • Behaving respectfully toward all staff and fellow patients
  • Paying all applicable fees before or at the time of service
  • Not misrepresenting your identity or using another person's identity for services
Failure to disclose critical medical information (such as metal implants before an MRI) that results in injury is the patient's responsibility. Our staff will conduct a safety screening before all imaging procedures.

Reports are prepared by our qualified radiologists and pathologists and are typically available within the timeframe communicated at booking.
